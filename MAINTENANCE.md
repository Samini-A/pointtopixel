# Dual-site maintenance

`pointypixel.com` and `pointtopixel.com` must remain live as separate sites for the transition period.

## Shared content

Changes to Dark Waves, game content, video, galleries, CV facts, and other shared material must be applied to both repositories:

- `Samini-A/pointypixel`
- `Samini-A/pointtopixel`

## Brand-specific content

Keep these different between the two repositories:

- Brand name: `Pointy Pixel` vs. `Point to Pixel`
- Domain: `pointypixel.com` vs. `pointtopixel.com`
- Email addresses for each domain
- Canonical and Open Graph URLs
- Brand wordmark assets

Do not redirect or retire `pointypixel.com` until the external dependency on that address has been removed.
